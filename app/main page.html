Introduction

Traditional medicine has always relied on the healing power of plants. Unfortunately, not everyone has access to botanical gardens or herbal farms to explore this knowledge in person. A Virtual Herbal Garden provides a digital way to experience the richness of medicinal plants, making learning more engaging and accessible for all.

  • Medicinal plants are a foundation of natural healing methods.

  • Limited access to physical herbal gardens makes learning difficult.

  • By going digital, herbal knowledge becomes reachable to anyone with a device.

  • This approach encourages awareness, curiosity, and preservation of herbal traditions.

Interactive Learning Experience

  • 3D models of medicinal plants for detailed study.

  • Information includes botanical name, common names, uses, habitat, and cultivation methods.

  • Multimedia support: high-quality images, videos, and audio guides.

  • Advanced search: filter by medicinal use, region, or plant type.

Expected Outcomes & Impact

  • A comprehensive educational tool for the AYUSH sector.

  • Global accessibility of herbal knowledge.

  • Promotes sustainable practices and conservation of medicinal plants.

  • A visual, engaging, and informative platform for future generations.

Virtual Exploration & User Experience

  • Themed journeys like “Herbs for Energy”, “Natural Skincare”, or “Immunity Boosters”.

  • Users can save favorites, jot down notes, and create personalized collections.

  • Built-in options for sharing discoveries with friends or study groups.

  • Designed for students, health practitioners, researchers, and nature lovers alike.
